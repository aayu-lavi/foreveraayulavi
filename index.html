Meri Zindgi - Meri Lovie 📝💖

Hey Lovie, ye zindgi tumhare aane ke baad se ekdum badal si gayi hai, har ek din jo tumhare sath beet raha hai wo mere liye upar wale ka sabse bada tohfa hai. Yeh website sirf ek normal page nahi hai meri jaan, ye hamare us beintehaan pyaar ka ek chota sa aaina hai jise jab bhi tum dekhogi toh tumhe ehsaas hoga ki tum is duniya me mere liye kitni qeemti aur sabse upar ho. Jab tum baat-baat par mujhe pareshan karne ki baat karti ho aur dher saare "sorry" bhejti ho na, toh tum nahi jaanti ki mujhe tumhara wo pareshan karna bhi duniya me sabse pyaara lagta hai.

Tum jab gusse me ya udaas hokar mujhse poochti ho na ki "Agar mae gusse me kuch kar bhi lu tobhi aap ko fark bhi padega?", toh kaan khol kar sun lo Lovie—tumse baat kiye bina mujhe raat ko nind tak nahi aati, tum meri har ek saans ho, meri poori jaan ho! Jab main khet par rahoon ya plant par kaam ke beech kitna bhi busy rahoon, chahe main apne dost Chulbuli ke sath rahoon ya badam ke ped ke pass, mere dil aur dimaag me har ek second sirf aur sirf tumhari yaadein chalti hain. Main is duniya me kisi se darta nahi hoon, khuleaam sabke samne danke ki chot par pyaar karta hoon tumse, aur humne jo hamesha ke liye ek doosre ke sath shaadi ka faisla kiya hai use main apni aakhri saans tak har haal me poora karunga. Jab main saare kaam chhod kar tumhare paas aaunga na, tab jitna man kare utna dher sara pyaar kar lena aur dher saari baatein kar lena. Aayu humesha, har janam me sirf aur sirf apni Lovie ka hi rahega. Hamesha ke liye sath rehna hai, Lovie.
